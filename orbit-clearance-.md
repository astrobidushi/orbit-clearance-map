# Orbit Clearance Map

Interactive teaching tool for EMGT 5061 (Introduction to Space Industry), U.S. Regulatory Environment session. Students pick one of three fictional satellite missions (Monsoon Radar, Beacon Mobile, Kestrel Orbital) and explore the U.S. regulatory and licensing path each one needs.

Open `index.html` in any browser, or view it through GitHub Pages.

- All content (missions, steps, curveballs, sources, glossary) is in the labeled CONTENT section near the top of the script in `index.html`.
- Missions are fictional composites modeled on real ones. Content reflects U.S. rules as of September 2026 and is a teaching aid, not legal advice.
